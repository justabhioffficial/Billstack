# BillStack Product Specification & Launch Roadmap

> **Product Vision**: "Turn photos and digital bills into clean, organized, tax-ready expense reports."

---

## 🎯 Target Audience
Primary: Indian freelancers, independent developers, designers, content creators, tutors, photographers, and solo consultants managing 10–200 receipts per month.
